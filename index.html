Integrative Oncology Support

A cancer diagnosis affects the whole person — body, mind, and spirit. At AWAREmed, integrative oncology support is supportive care designed to work alongside the treatment your oncology team provides, never in place of it. Our focus is on helping you feel as strong and well-supported as possible throughout your journey.

Led by Dr. Dalal Akoury, MD, this program may address nutrition, energy, comfort, stress, and overall well-being. We coordinate respectfully with your treating physicians and keep your safety at the center of every decision.

Please note: AWAREmed does not diagnose or treat cancer and does not replace oncology care. Our role is supportive and complementary. Always continue care with your oncology team.
How We May Help

What this support may include

Every plan is personalized to your history, goals, and clinical findings. Depending on what is appropriate for you, care may include:

  • Nutritional guidance designed to support strength and well-being during treatment
  • Lifestyle and stress-support strategies to help you cope with the demands of treatment
  • IV hydration and nutrient support where clinically appropriate and coordinated with your team
  • Education about evidence-informed integrative options so you can make confident decisions
  • Whole-person support addressing sleep, energy, and emotional well-being
  • Care coordinated respectfully with your oncologist and treatment plan

IV Therapy & Hydration

IV (intravenous) therapy delivers fluids, vitamins, and nutrients directly into the bloodstream. Because it bypasses the digestive system, IV delivery can support hydration and nutrient levels efficiently. At AWAREmed, every IV is reviewed and supervised by a physician and tailored to your individual needs.

We design each blend after understanding your history and goals, so your therapy is appropriate for you rather than a one-size-fits-all drip.

Please note: IV therapy is intended to support hydration and wellness. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Appropriateness is determined during a clinical evaluation.
How We May Help

What this support may include

Every plan is personalized to your history, goals, and clinical findings. Depending on what is appropriate for you, care may include:

  • Hydration support for everyday wellness, travel, or recovery
  • Vitamin and nutrient blends personalized to your needs
  • Support for energy and general well-being
  • Comfortable, unhurried sessions in a calm clinic setting
  • Physician oversight of formulation and safety screening
  • Coordination with your broader care plan when relevant

Please note: Hormone evaluation and therapy are individualized and require clinical assessment. This page is educational and is not a diagnosis or a treatment recommendation.

Gut Health & Functional Medicine

Dr. Akoury uses a personalized, root-cause approach — combining history, appropriate testing, nutrition, and lifestyle support designed for you.

Please note: This page is educational. Digestive symptoms can have many causes; a clinical evaluation is needed to determine an appropriate, individualized approach.
