FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Functional medicine is a patient-centered approach that seeks to identify and address the root causes of disease. Instead of managing symptoms with medications, functional medicine practitioners look at the interplay between genetics, environment, and lifestyle. Dr. Akoury takes a comprehensive history, orders advanced lab testing, and creates personalised protocols that address the underlying drivers of your condition — not just the symptoms.

AWAREmed operates as a cash-pay and membership-based practice for most integrative services. This model allows Dr. Akoury to spend the time with each patient that comprehensive integrative care requires — without the limitations imposed by insurance reimbursement structures. Some conventional laboratory tests may be partially covered by insurance. Please contact our office for specific insurance questions: (423) 430-6170.

Initial consultations with Dr. Akoury typically last 60–90 minutes. This extended time allows her to conduct a thorough review of your complete health history, symptoms, lifestyle, diet, stress, and goals. Most patients comment that this is the most comprehensive health conversation they've ever had with a physician. Follow-up appointments are typically 30–45 minutes.

IV therapy sessions typically last 30–90 minutes depending on the infusion type. You'll be comfortably seated in our infusion suite. Most patients feel an energizing effect during or shortly after their session. Common IV formulas include the Myers' Cocktail (energy, immunity), High-Dose Vitamin C (antioxidant, immune), NAD+ (cognitive clarity, anti-aging), and Glutathione (detoxification). Dr. Akoury recommends the most appropriate protocol based on your individual health needs.

Bioidentical hormone therapy (BHRT), when prescribed and monitored by a qualified physician, has an excellent safety profile for appropriate candidates. Bioidentical hormones are structurally identical to the hormones naturally produced by your body, which differentiates them from synthetic hormone preparations. Dr. Akoury conducts thorough baseline testing, monitors hormone levels regularly, and adjusts dosing precisely to maintain optimal — not supraphysiologic — levels. Any therapy involves individual risk-benefit assessment, which Dr. Akoury discusses in detail during your consultation.

Yes, AWAREmed offers telemedicine consultations for established patients and for initial consultations where in-person examination is not immediately required. Lab testing can be coordinated through local labs in your area. Telemedicine allows Dr. Akoury to serve patients throughout Tennessee and beyond. Please contact us to discuss whether a virtual consultation is appropriate for your specific needs.

Results vary depending on the condition being treated, its severity, and how long it has been present. Many patients report noticeable improvements in energy, mood, and symptoms within 4–8 weeks of beginning a comprehensive protocol. IV therapy often produces results within 24–48 hours. Hormone optimization typically takes 6–12 weeks for full effect. Chronic conditions that have developed over years may require 6–12 months of comprehensive treatment for full resolution. Dr. Akoury will give you a realistic timeline based on your specific situation during your initial consultation.
